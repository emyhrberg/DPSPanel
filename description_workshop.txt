[h1] DPSPanel [/h1]
Ever wondered who did the most damage in a boss fight? And by which weapons they did it with? Now you can! DPSPanel provides a intuitive combat analysis of damage done by each player. It tracks, sorts, and displays the damage done by player and weapon.

[h2] Usage [/h2]
With the mod enabled, a toggle button will appear which you can use to toggle the visibility of the panel, open config, clear boss fight, and more. When a boss fight starts, the tracking will automatically begin!
